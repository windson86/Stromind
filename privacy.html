2. Data we process

Applications

When you apply, we may process your name, email address, telephone number, professional experience, availability and preferred work area, position information, messages, answers and score from the practical check, and a CV that you upload voluntarily.

Please upload only documents needed for the application. In particular, do not use the application form to send copies of identity documents, bank details, medical records, or information about religion, political opinions, trade-union membership, sexual orientation or other special categories of personal data unless we expressly and lawfully request them.

Project enquiries from companies

For project enquiries we may process the company name, contact person, business contact details, project location and timeframe, requested number and profiles of skilled workers, and the content of the enquiry.

Website use

When the website is accessed, technically necessary connection and log data may be processed, including IP address, time of access, requested resource, browser/device information and security-related events. Your selected website language may be stored locally in your browser so that the site can remember your choice.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Application and recruitment/placement process: handling the application, contacting you, professional assessment and steps requested by you before entering into a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Documentation and legal defence: where necessary, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is to document the selection process and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Project and business enquiries: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual steps or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for legitimate business communication.
  • Technical operation and security: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is a secure, stable and abuse-resistant website.
  • Legal obligations: where applicable, Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

The checkbox confirming that this privacy notice has been read is not consent to processing that is necessary to handle the specific application. It documents that the information required by Article 13 GDPR was made available.

4. Required information

Fields marked as required are necessary for us to handle the relevant application or enquiry. Without them, we may not be able to process the request. In general, there is no separate legal obligation to provide data through the website form.

5. Practical check and automated processing

Some applications include a short practical check with five technical questions. The answers are technically converted into a score from 0 to 5. The score is used only to support human professional review. An application is not accepted or rejected solely because of this score. STROMIND does not use solely automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.

6. Recipients and processors

Access is limited to persons who need the data for the relevant purpose. In operating the service, the following providers may process data:

  • Netlify, Inc. – website hosting and, when enabled, processing of web forms and uploaded files as a processor.
  • STRATO AG – business email service.
  • IT, legal, tax or other advisers where necessary and legally permitted.
  • Authorities or courts where disclosure is required by law.

A CV or applicant profile is shared with a specific potential employer or business partner only where this is necessary for the specific recruitment/placement process and is transparent to the applicant. STROMIND does not place applicants in a general talent pool or send profiles to unspecified third parties without a separate lawful basis where one is required.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Netlify is a provider based in the United States, so processing outside the European Economic Area may occur. Such transfers rely on the safeguards provided in Netlify's Data Processing Agreement, including Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, an adequacy mechanism. Before public launch, the DPA version applying to the STROMIND account will be documented.

8. Retention

  • Unsuccessful applications: generally up to six months after the relevant selection/placement process ends or after the last substantive contact, unless a longer period is required by law or needed for legal claims.
  • Netlify form copy: after transfer into the controlled recruitment workflow, the Netlify form submission is deleted on a regular basis and no later than 30 days. The main recruitment record may remain in the designated business systems for the period stated above.
  • Successful applications: necessary data is moved into the relevant employment, contract or placement documentation and retained under the applicable legal retention periods.
  • Business enquiries: for as long as necessary for handling the enquiry, the business relationship and applicable legal retention/limitation periods.
  • Technical logs: only for as long as needed for operation and security.

9. Special categories of data

STROMIND does not intentionally collect special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR through its ordinary web forms. If such information is sent unsolicited, it will only be processed where an appropriate legal basis exists and the processing is necessary; otherwise it will be removed as part of data-cleaning procedures.

10. Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and the right to object to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where processing is exceptionally based on consent, consent can be withdrawn for the future.

To exercise your rights, contact info@stromind.de.

11. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority. For the controller's Croatian establishment, the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka – AZOP) is the relevant authority. You may also contact another supervisory authority competent under the GDPR.

12. Cookies, tracking and external content

The planned public launch does not use analytics or marketing trackers. The language choice is stored only as a functional browser preference. External services that are not required for tracking or advertising should not be embedded in the application and project-enquiry forms.

13. Changes

This notice will be updated when services, purposes of processing or legal requirements change.
