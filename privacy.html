2. Data we process

Applications

When you apply, we process data including your name, email address, telephone number, professional experience, position applied for, optional message and – for industrial electrician/installer applications – your answers and the resulting practical-check score. You may optionally upload a CV in PDF or DOCX format up to 3.5 MB.

Please send only documents relevant to the application. In particular, do not submit copies of identity documents, bank details, medical records, or information about religion, political opinions, trade-union membership, sexual orientation or other special categories of personal data unless STROMIND expressly and lawfully requests them.

Project enquiries from companies

For project enquiries we process data including company name, contact person, business contact details, project location and timeframe, requested number and profiles of skilled workers, and the content of the enquiry.

Website use

When the website is accessed, technically necessary connection and log data may be processed, including IP address, access time, requested resource, browser/device information and security-related events. The selected website language may be stored locally in the browser.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Applications: Where an application concerns a possible contract or employment relationship with STROMIND, processing is carried out to take steps at the applicant's request prior to entering into a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where STROMIND acts for a specific third party or intends to disclose an applicant profile to a specific potential employer, the legal basis appropriate to the actual placement process will be determined before that disclosure and the applicant will be informed transparently about the recipient and purpose.
  • Documentation and legal defence: where necessary, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is proper documentation of the selection process and the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
  • Project and business enquiries: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual steps or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for legitimate business communication.
  • Technical operation and security: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is a secure, stable and abuse-resistant website.
  • Legal obligations: where applicable, Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

The checkbox confirming that this privacy notice has been read is not consent to processing that is already necessary on another lawful basis for the specific application or enquiry. It records that the information required by Article 13 GDPR was made available.

4. Required information

Fields marked as required are necessary for us to handle the application or enquiry. Without them we may be unable to process the request. In general, there is no separate legal obligation to provide data specifically through the web form.

5. Practical check and automated processing

Industrial electrician/installer applications include five technical questions. The answers are converted server-side into a score from 0 to 5. The score is used only to support human professional review. An application is not automatically accepted, rejected or discarded solely because of the score. STROMIND does not use solely automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.

6. Recipients and processors

Access is limited to persons who need the data for the relevant purpose. The following service providers are used for technical operation:

  • Netlify, Inc. – website hosting. Applications are technically received and validated by a Netlify Function and then forwarded through the configured mail service. The intended application workflow does not store CVs as Netlify Forms files. Company project enquiries may, once Netlify Forms is enabled, be processed as Netlify form submissions.
  • STRATO AG – business email service. Application data and any optional CV submitted through the application form are delivered to the configured STROMIND business mailbox and stored there as part of the application process.
  • IT, legal, tax and other advisers where necessary and legally permitted.
  • Authorities or courts where disclosure is required by law.

A CV or applicant profile is disclosed to a specific potential employer or business partner only where this is necessary for the concrete process, an appropriate lawful basis exists and the applicant has been informed transparently about the recipient or recipient category and purpose. STROMIND does not generally distribute profiles to unspecified third parties or maintain a general talent pool without separately assessing the lawful basis.

7. Processing outside the EEA

Netlify is a provider based in the United States. Technical processing of an application by the Netlify Function may take place outside the European Economic Area depending on the Functions region actually configured for the STROMIND account and the Netlify services used. Before public launch, STROMIND will document the actual Functions region, the Netlify Data Processing Agreement version applicable to the account and the transfer mechanisms it provides. Where a third-country transfer occurs, the required safeguards, including the Standard Contractual Clauses provided in the DPA or an applicable adequacy mechanism, will be relied upon as appropriate.

8. Retention

  • Unsuccessful applications: application data and CVs are deleted when no longer required for the application/placement process and any necessary legal defence. As an organisational maximum, deletion is generally intended no later than six months after the concrete process ends or the last substantive contact, unless a documented statutory or legal need justifies longer retention.
  • Netlify for applications: the intended workflow does not create an applicant record or store a CV as a Netlify Forms submission. The Function processes the request for validation and forwarding; technically necessary operational/security metadata may be processed under Netlify's applicable retention periods.
  • Company project enquiries: where Netlify Forms is enabled for these enquiries, Netlify copies should be deleted regularly and no later than 30 days after transfer into the controlled business workflow unless there is a concrete need for longer retention.
  • Successful applications: necessary data is transferred to the relevant employment, contract or placement records and retained under the applicable statutory periods.
  • Business enquiries: for as long as required for handling the enquiry, the business relationship and applicable statutory retention/limitation periods.

9. Special categories of data

STROMIND does not intentionally collect special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR through its ordinary web forms. If such information is sent unsolicited, it is processed only where an appropriate lawful basis exists and the processing is necessary; otherwise it is removed as part of data-cleaning procedures.

10. Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and the right to object to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where processing is exceptionally based on consent, consent can be withdrawn for the future.

To exercise your rights, contact info@stromind.de.

11. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority. For the controller's establishment in Croatia, the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka – AZOP) is particularly relevant. You may also contact another supervisory authority competent under the GDPR.

12. Cookies, tracking and external content

The intended public launch does not use analytics or marketing trackers. The language choice is stored only as a functional browser preference. The application and project-enquiry forms should not load unnecessary advertising or tracking services.

13. Changes

This notice will be updated if service providers, technical flows, processing purposes, legal bases or statutory requirements change.
